What is BlackLight?

BlackLight® is a discovery tool that helps you find Black-owned businesses while you search and shop online.

It works through a browser extension and companion web app that highlight Black-owned businesses directly within your search experience, making it easier to discover and support them.

How does it work?

BlackLight® helps you discover Black-owned businesses while you search and shop online.

On desktop, install the BlackLight browser extension from the Chrome Web Store or the Microsoft Edge Add-ons store. Search the web as you normally would. When Black-owned businesses match your search, BlackLight highlights them directly in your results so you can easily discover and support them.

On mobile, simply visit blacklightapp.io to explore the BlackLight web app on your phone and discover Black-owned businesses wherever you are.

Our database of businesses is continually growing as we expand the number of Black-owned businesses you can discover while you search and shop online.

Isn’t there something like this already out there?

There are directories and apps that list Black-owned businesses, but they usually require you to visit a separate site or remember to search within their platform.

BlackLight® works differently. It integrates directly into the way you already search and shop online. When Black-owned businesses match your search, BlackLight displays them right below the search bar at the top of the page—even above paid results—so they’re easy to discover.

Instead of changing your habits, BlackLight lets you search the way you always do while making it simple to find and support Black-owned businesses.

Where do I get BlackLight®?

You can install the BlackLight browser extension from the Chrome Web Store or the Microsoft Edge Add-ons store.

If you're using a mobile device, simply visit blacklightapp.io to explore the BlackLight web app and discover Black-owned businesses on your phone.

Which browsers does BlackLight work with?

BlackLight® currently works with Google Chrome and Microsoft Edge through their browser extension stores.

On mobile devices, you can visit blacklightapp.io to use the BlackLight companion web app, which works on iPhones and other smartphones.

Do I need an account to use BlackLight?

No. You can use the BlackLight® browser extension without creating an account.

If you use the BlackLight web app, creating an account allows you to access additional features such as favorites and recent history. These features require a secure login so your preferences can be saved.

What about privacy?

We take privacy seriously. The BlackLight browser extension does not require an account and does not collect personal information from your searches.

If you choose to create an account in the BlackLight web app, we collect basic information such as your name, email address, and password so you can use features like favorites and recent history.

We do not sell your personal information. Our Privacy Policy and Terms of Use follow applicable laws and industry best practices to protect your information.

Why should I shop Black-owned?

Supporting Black-owned businesses helps expand economic opportunity and strengthen communities. Redirecting everyday spending toward these businesses helps entrepreneurs grow, creates jobs, and contributes to closing long-standing wealth gaps.

It’s also a great way to discover unique products, services, and innovative brands you might not encounter otherwise.

How are businesses added to BlackLight?

Businesses in the BlackLight database are identified using information from publicly available sources such as government records, local business directories, and other online listings.

Our proprietary workflow collects and standardizes this information, uses AI to help categorize businesses, and checks for duplicate entries before adding or updating listings in the BlackLight database.

While we rely on credible data sources, BlackLight does not independently verify business ownership, as many public records allow ownership information to be self-reported.

How will this help my business?

BlackLight® makes it easier for people who want to support Black-owned businesses to discover yours while they search and shop online.

By being included in the BlackLight network, your business can gain visibility with consumers actively looking to support Black-owned brands, as well as organizations seeking diverse suppliers and partners.

If you’re a Black-owned business interested in being included, contact the BlackLight team for next steps.
